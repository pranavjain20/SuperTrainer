# Day 2 — Feb 20, 2026

**Rewrote the product direction. Rebuilt all documentation from scratch.**

## The product pivot

Yesterday we built a working v1 backend — 93 tests, 7 models, 15 endpoints. Today we stepped back and changed what we're building and why.

The v1 PRD was framed around monetization: billing tiers, subscription flows, payment infrastructure woven into the architecture from day one. After reflecting on it, Pranav decided that was optimizing for the wrong thing at the wrong stage. The new framing: SuperTrainer is a portfolio and research artifact first. A real product that demonstrates deep understanding of AI + fitness, with genuine utility for trainers. If trainers love it and it grows, monetization follows. The goal for the next 2-2.5 months is to build something that real trainers use and say helped them — not something with a payment form.

This changed what features get prioritized, how the intelligence layer is designed, and what "done" means.

## What changed in the data model

The biggest structural shift: **ExerciseLog replaced by SessionEntry.**

In v1, a session was a list of exercises. But trainers think in moments, not exercises. "She did 4 sets of squats, then I noticed her knee tracking inward, then we switched to leg press." That's a timeline of mixed events. SessionEntry is a unified model with `entry_type` (exercise_card | observation_card). Sessions become timelines, not exercise lists.

This touches every layer — models, schemas, routes, services, tests, seed data. No way to incrementally migrate, so we committed to a clean rebuild.

Other new models:
- **SessionPlan** — plans as first-class entities, linkable to sessions via FK
- **BrainConversation + BrainMessage** — persistent conversation history for The Brain (conversational AI agent)
- **pgvector embeddings** on text-heavy fields for future semantic search

Updated models:
- **Session** gains `scheduled_for` and `plan_id` FK
- **ClientAnalysis** gains `client_score` and `score_breakdown`
- **InjuryFlag** FK changes from `exercise_log_id` to `session_entry_id`

## Feature reprioritization

- Billing/subscriptions pushed out of scope for initial build
- The Brain (conversational agent) promoted to core — trainer asks "how has Marcus's knee been trending?" and gets an intelligent, data-grounded answer
- Pre-session briefings redesigned with data maturity gates: new client gets observations, long-term client gets trend analysis and risk alerts. The AI doesn't overstate what the data supports.
- Voice pipeline changed from batch upload to per-clip real-time processing

## What I learned

- Working code in the wrong direction is harder to course-correct than no code. 93 passing tests created momentum, but the underlying model was wrong for what we actually want to build. Better to catch this now than after 300 tests.
- Speccing everything before coding eliminates ambiguity. Tomorrow's session starts with every field, relationship, and endpoint defined. No design decisions left to make during implementation.
