# Day 12 — Phase 2a Complete

## What got built

Two features from yesterday's testing notes, then the merge.

**Active session banner.** When you're recording and navigate away — check another client, go to Home, whatever — there's now a persistent banner at the top of the tab bar: "Recording with Jake Morrison · 12:34." Tap it, you're back in the session. No more "where did my session go?" The trainer should never lose track of what they're doing, regardless of where they wander.

**Clarification UX.** When the parser can't understand a clip, it no longer pretends to be a client observation. There's a `ClarificationModal` that pops up — "didn't catch that, want to try again or type it?" Plus a `ManualEntryModal` for when voice just isn't going to work (loud gym, specific terminology). The timeline renders these as distinct system messages, not yellow cards. A trainer glancing at their phone mid-set can instantly tell the difference between "something about my client" and "the app needs my help."

Backend got updated too — the parser now returns confidence metadata, the voice service handles a `clarification_needed` status, and 207 new tests cover the clarification flows. 734 total backend tests.

**The merge.** Squash merged 19 commits (Days 1-12) into master as a single commit. 96 files, 21k lines. Feature branch deleted. Clean.

## Phase 2a in retrospect

12 days. Zero React Native experience to a working app on my phone. I don't understand the architecture deeply yet — couldn't explain how Expo's file-based routing works under the hood, couldn't debug a NativeWind config from scratch. But I know how to test on the phone now. I know what to look for. I'm more fluent than I was, more educated. That's real progress.

The biggest lesson from this entire phase: you use and you learn. Use and learn. You can't think of these things beforehand. The active session banner, the clarification UX, the "Session means nothing as a workout type" bug, the typography hierarchy — none of that came from specs or planning. It all came from holding the phone and feeling what was wrong. There's no other way to build product. You can't spec your way to good UX.

# Day 11 — Phase 2a Phone Testing

## What happened

First phone testing session. Ran all 7 phases on the physical iPhone — every flow works end-to-end. Recording, editing, ending sessions, navigation guards, edge cases. 12 fixes came out of it, fixed live as I found them.

## What testing surfaced

You can't think of these things on your own naturally. Testing helps so much. The moment I'm holding the phone, stuff jumps out.

**"Session" means nothing.** The workout type field was showing "Session" for every session. Turned out the classifier had a bug — seed data stores `goblet_squat` but the exercise DB has `Goblet Squat`. One-line fix, but I never would've noticed without seeing it on screen.

**Sets in the summary don't matter.** Duration and exercise count is enough at a glance. Sets is noise.

**Exercise names were bigger than the summary.** The hierarchy was off — exercises at 17px were competing with the summary section above them. Dropped to 15px, immediately felt right.

**Yellow highlighting on set notes — too much.** Observations already handle color-coded flags. Having yellow rows inside the set table on top of that was just visual clutter. Stripped it.

**"Done" and "In Progress" were both green.** That's confusing. Changed In Progress to orange — now you can instantly tell the difference.

**Grey observations don't make sense.** I asked myself: can I think of a neutral observation? Honestly, no. If I'm saying something mid-session, it's either good ("great form"), something to watch ("energy is low"), or concerning ("lower back pain"). There's no neutral. Made flag color required — green, yellow, or red on everything.

**Navigating away from an active session is disorienting.** I tapped back mid-session and ended up on the client profile. As a trainer, I was confused — where's my session? I had to figure out to go to the Home tab and tap "Continue Session." There should be a persistent banner across all screens when a session is active, like how Uber shows your active ride everywhere.

**The AI "didn't understand" case is wrong.** When the parser can't understand a voice clip, it creates a yellow observation card. But that looks identical to a real observation about my client. Mid-session, I'm coaching someone — I glance at my phone and see a yellow card with "Unclear reference." I don't know what that means. My brain is on my client, not on debugging AI output. It needs to be a completely different visual — a system message, not a client observation.
